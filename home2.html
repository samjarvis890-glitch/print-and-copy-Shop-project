MATERIAL INTELLIGENCE

Paper makes all the difference in your hands.

The weight, texture, and brightness of paper transform how a reader perceives your information. Explore the core variables before choosing your stock.

Paper Size

A4 & US Letter: Universal standard for office paperwork and reports.
A3 & Tabloid: Ideal for schematics, financial spreadsheets, and posters.
A5 Booklets: Compact pocket guides and event programs.

Paper Weight (GSM)

80gsm: Everyday standard copy bond.
100–120gsm: Smooth premium stationery, zero show-through.
250–350gsm: Sturdy cardstock for covers, cards, and invites.

Surface & Finish

Uncoated Bond: Natural tooth, effortless to write on.
Silk Matte: Non-glare, velvety touch, sharp readability.
Gloss Coated: Vibrant colors and rich photograph depth.

Intended Use & Stock

Everyday Utility: Quick copies and internal packets.
Executive Presentation: Heavy covers, premium bind.
In-Stock Guarantee: Common weights kept ready at our counter.

Counter Swatch Inspection: Feel physical weight swatches at our front desk anytime before finalizing your full print order.

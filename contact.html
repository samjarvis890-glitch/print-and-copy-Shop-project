Preparation Guide

BEFORE YOU CONTACT US

Having these five project details in mind helps our printing specialists understand your exact request and calculate accurate job costs promptly.

01

What do you need?

Describe whether you need document printing, photocopying, booklet binding, or laminated presentation sheets.

02

How many copies?

Share the approximate number of pages per document, and how many duplicate sets or finished copies are required.

03

Which paper or print type?

Mention paper size (A4, A3, letter) and whether you require calibrated CMYK color or monochrome black-and-white.

04

Is finishing required?

Mention wire binding, plastic comb coils, corner stapling, hole-punching, or laminating, subject to availability.

05

Is there anything else to know?

Add any special instructions such as strict deadline dates, custom packaging, or courier collection timing.

Not sure about the specifications?

Do not worry if you do not know every detail yet. Provide what you know, and our printing specialists will guide you through the remaining choices when reviewing your enquiry.

  • Paper sample books available at our front desk
  • Pre-flight file checking for margin and bleed
  • Binding thickness recommendation upon request

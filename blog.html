PRODUCTION DISCIPLINE

The Anatomy of a Print Job

A disciplined, three-stage editorial workflow ensures every file transitions smoothly from digital layout to crisp physical print.

STAGE 01 Pre-Flight

Prepare the Document

Before hitting print, inspect page dimensions, layout orientation, and safety margins. Verify that high-resolution vector assets are linked and that no text sits within 5mm of the trim edge.

  • Standardize page size (A4, A3, Letter) and avoid scaled distortions
  • Ensure 3mm minimum bleed for edge-to-edge artwork
  • Embed all typography and flatten complex transparency layers
STAGE 02 Substrate & Mode

Confirm the Requirements

Match the physical attributes to the document's life span. Confirm paper weight (GSM), surface finish (matte or silk), single or double-sided tumble direction, and exact quantity.

  • Select appropriate GSM weight for handling frequency and rigidity
  • Confirm duplex orientation (flip on long vs short edge)
  • Specify binding style early to accommodate spine margin allowances
STAGE 03 Quality Audit

Review the Result

Examine the first printed sheet or available sample before proceeding with larger volume runs. Check ink density, color balance, fold registration, and trim crispness.

  • Verify text crispness and contrast against paper brightness
  • Inspect mechanical trim alignment across multi-page collations
  • Test lay-flat functionality on bound sample booklets
MATERIAL JOURNAL

A Closer Look at Paper

Paper selection depends on the job, equipment, and intended purpose. Explore the four tactile pillars that define every printed piece.

01

Size & Proportion

Choose dimensions suitable for the intended document: standard A4/A3 for operational documents, or custom trims for bespoke menus.

02

Weight & Thickness

Consider handling requirements: lightweight 80gsm for mailings, 120gsm for smooth opacity, and 300gsm+ for sturdy cover stock.

03

Surface & Texture

Understand how texture and smoothness influence presentation: toothy uncoated stock absorbs notes, while calendered sheets sharpen type.

04

Finish & Protection

Evaluate final durability: glare-free silk matte for reading comfort, gloss for vibrant photographs, and laminates for moisture protection.

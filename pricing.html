Information to Provide for Your Copying Quote

  • Black-and-White vs. Color: High-contrast crisp monochrome copying or calibrated full-color laser duplication.
  • Total Page Count & Sets: Number of master pages plus how many duplicate sets are required.
  • Orientation & Paper Size: Standard A4, larger A3, or custom sizes; single-sided (simplex) or double-sided (duplex).
  • Finishing & Collation: Corner stapling, 2/4-hole punching, or slip-sheet dividers between sets.

WHAT SHAPES YOUR FINAL QUOTE?

Understand how our print specialists evaluate your project details to formulate an accurate, honest quotation without arbitrary surcharges.

Print Requirements + Materials + Quantity + Finishing + Job Details = Quotation
01

Print Requirements

Color coverage versus monochrome grayscale, single-sided or double-sided impressions, and text density.

02

Materials

Standard paper sheet sizes (A4, A3, custom), grammage weight (80gsm to 350gsm), and surface texture.

03

Quantity

Total page count per document, number of duplicate sets, and volume scale advantages for larger runs.

04

Finishing

Wire or spiral binding, corner stapling, hole punching, folding, collating, or protective lamination.

05

Job Details

Print-ready PDF formatting, bleed alignment, turnaround urgency, and specialized handling instructions.

Quotations should be confirmed with our counter team before committing to an order to verify file readiness and turnaround schedules.

PREPARE YOUR PRINT QUOTE SPECIFICATIONS

Gather these eight key project specifications before requesting your price to ensure the fastest and most accurate estimate possible.

Customer Specification Checklist

Informational guide for requesting rapid commercial quotations
Reference Guide
Field 01

What do you need printed or copied?

E.g., corporate booklets, architectural blueprints, flyers, study reports, or certificates.

Field 02

What is the paper size?

Standard A4 (210×297mm), A3 (297×420mm), US Letter, or custom millimeter dimensions.

Field 03

Is the job color or black-and-white?

Full CMYK color, monochrome grayscale, or specific mixed color insert sheets.

Field 04

How many pages and copies are required?

Total page count per master document, and the exact count of duplicate copy sets.

Field 05

Is printing single-sided or double-sided?

Simplex (front face only) or duplex (front and back with gutter margins).

Field 06

Is binding or finishing required?

Twin-loop wire, plastic comb, corner stapling, hole punching, or loose collated sheets.

Field 07

Do you have a print-ready file?

High-resolution PDF, 300 DPI image assets, fonts embedded, and optional 3mm bleed.

Field 08

Are there any special instructions?

Specific deadline requirements, packaging preferences, or courier collection.

Bring these details to our front counter or submit them via our online enquiry form.

Pre-Flight Best Practices

  • Export documents as PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4 for reliable color reproduction.
  • Ensure full-bleed artwork extends at least 3mm beyond the final page boundary.
  • Leave at least 10mm inner margin if choosing wire or comb binding.
